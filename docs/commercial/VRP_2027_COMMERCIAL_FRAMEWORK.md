## 1. Executive Statement

Veil Routing Protocol (VRP) is an independently
developed networking and session continuity technology.

VRP follows a fundamental architectural principle:

**SESSION ≠ TRANSPORT**

VRP is designed to address complex networking challenges,
including:

- Session continuity across transport changes
- Network mobility and roaming
- Transport migration and recovery
- Authority and generation management
- Replay and stale-state rejection
- Failure containment
- Deterministic validation
- Independently verifiable evidence

VRP is not positioned as a conventional consumer VPN.

Its commercial model targets organizations requiring
specialized networking technology, engineering expertise,
and controlled access to a protected runtime.

---

## 2. Commercial Positioning

Beginning January 1, 2027, VRP adopts a proposed
premium enterprise licensing framework.

Pricing is based on:

- Technology evaluation scope
- Deployment scale
- Engineering involvement
- Integration complexity
- Support requirements
- Commercial licensing rights
- Strategic partnership conditions

VRP does not compete primarily on subscription price.

The objective is to establish commercial relationships
based on technical value, measurable validation,
and long-term engineering cooperation.

All prices are indicative until confirmed
in a signed commercial agreement.

## 8. Pilot-to-Production Transition

A successful pilot does not automatically
authorize production deployment.

Production licensing requires:

1. Completed technical evaluation
2. Agreed acceptance criteria
3. Deployment compatibility assessment
4. Security and operational review
5. Commercial agreement
6. Executed production license

Pilot fees are not automatically refundable
or credited toward annual licensing.

Any pilot-to-production credit must be
expressly agreed in writing.

## 9. Engineering Validation

VRP follows an evidence-oriented
engineering approach.

Potential validation areas include:

- Session identity preservation
- Transport migration
- Network interruption
- NAT rebinding
- Replay rejection
- Authority generation fencing
- Recovery behavior
- Concurrent operations
- Evidence integrity
- Failure containment

Individual capabilities must be evaluated
against the applicable runtime version.

The following classifications apply:

**VERIFIED**

Supported by reproducible validation evidence.

**IN DEVELOPMENT**

Under active implementation or hardening.

**PROPOSED**

Planned capability or future engineering objective.

No unverified capability is represented
as a completed production guarantee.
