## 1. Purpose

This document describes the proposed commercial licensing principles for Veil Routing Protocol (VRP).

VRP is independently developed networking and session continuity technology.

Its architectural foundation is:

**SESSION ≠ TRANSPORT**

The purpose of commercial licensing is to provide organizations with defined rights to evaluate or use approved VRP components while preserving the intellectual property, security boundaries, and commercial independence of the technology.

These terms are a proposed framework.

They do not constitute a binding license agreement.

No commercial license is granted solely through publication of this document.

Binding rights and obligations arise only under an applicable executed agreement.

Permits controlled technical assessment of specified VRP components.

- Reviewing approved documentation

- Examining agreed validation evidence
- Conducting authorized integration feasibility assessments

- Research environments

- Runtime access
- Documentation access
- Evaluation duration

Commercial production use is excluded unless expressly authorized.

### 5.3 Business Runtime License

May permit defined commercial use of approved VRP runtime components.

Deployment limits, supported environments, usage rights, maintenance, and support must be established contractually.

### 5.4 Enterprise Runtime License

May permit broader deployment rights for eligible enterprise infrastructure.

The agreement may define:

- Authorized organizations
- Deployment locations
- Runtime instances
- Operational environments
- Integration boundaries
- Support arrangements
- Maintenance obligations
- Security requirements

May provide expanded contractual rights for complex enterprise programs.

Additional rights are negotiated individually.

Strategic licensing does not automatically include source-code ownership or exclusivity.

### 5.6 Exclusive Commercial License

Any exclusive rights require a separate express agreement.

- Industry

- Deployment environment

No exclusivity is granted by implication.
